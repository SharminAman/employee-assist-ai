CONTOSO SERVICES — EMPLOYEE HR POLICY

ANNUAL LEAVE

Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year in addition to UK bank holidays.

The holiday year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

Employees requesting five or more consecutive working days of annual leave should normally submit their request at least four weeks in advance.

For shorter periods of annual leave, employees should normally provide at least two weeks' notice.

Employees may carry over up to five unused annual leave days into the following year with manager approval. Carried-over leave should normally be used by 31 March.


SICKNESS ABSENCE

Employees who are unable to work because of illness should notify their line manager by 9:30 AM on the first day of absence.

Employees may self-certify sickness absence for up to seven calendar days.

If sickness absence lasts more than seven calendar days, the employee must provide a fit note.

A return-to-work discussion may be conducted when the employee returns.


HYBRID WORKING

Eligible employees may normally work remotely for up to two days per week.

Hybrid working arrangements remain subject to business requirements, team requirements and manager approval.

Requests for arrangements outside the standard hybrid-working policy should be discussed with the employee's manager or HR.


EXPENSES

Reasonable business expenses may be reimbursed where they are incurred for approved business purposes.

Expense claims should normally be submitted within 30 days and should include appropriate receipts.

Expense claims require manager approval.


HR SUPPORT AND ESCALATION

Employees should contact HR for complex or sensitive matters including grievances, disciplinary matters, harassment or discrimination concerns, reasonable adjustments and contractual disputes.

The HR assistant should not invent policies where information is unavailable.

If a policy cannot be confirmed from the available HR information, the employee should be advised to contact HR.
